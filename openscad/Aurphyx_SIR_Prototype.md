## Technical Manufacturing Notes for Vendor

1. **Machining Setup:** 5-axis CNC stone milling center equipped with diamond-sintered end mills or an ultrasonic/waterjet hybrid cutting system.
2. **Coolant Purity:** Water-only or non-staining synthetic lapidary coolant required. Oily or sulfur-based metal cutting fluids are strictly prohibited to prevent stone porosity staining.
3. **Structural Wall Integrity:** Minimum web thickness between cavity boundaries and external edges is 0.78 inches (19.8 mm). Feed rates must be regulated to prevent sub-surface micro-fracturing along cavity floors.
