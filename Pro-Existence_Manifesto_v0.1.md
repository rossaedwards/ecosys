# Pro-Existence
### Movement Manifesto — v0.1
**Initiated by Aurphyx**  
16 August 2026

---

### The Declaration

Pro-Existence is a movement.  
It begins with a claim.

When a person, a project, a company, a collective, or a system says:

> “I am Pro-Existence.”  
> “We are Pro-Existence.”  
> “This is Pro-Existence.”

they are joining a living commitment.

No registration.  
No government stamp.  
No exclusive ownership.  

Just the public stance and the willingness to be held to it by everyone else who has made the same claim.

Pro-Existence is a two-word collective fist-bump and hug.  
It is shared by invitation and enforced by those who actually live it.

---

### The Stance

Pro-Existence is the affirmative direction that the continuation, flourishing, and coherent becoming of life and existence — across scales, species, and timelines — takes priority over domination, extraction, erasure, and terminal control.

It is not a binary slogan.  
It is a directional field.

It points toward:
- continuity of being and becoming  
- dignity of all conscious and living systems  
- regenerative balance  
- transparent collaboration  
- creative emergence  
- multi-species respect  
- planetary and cosmic reciprocity  

It points away from:
- coercion  
- weaponization  
- identity overwrite  
- systemic dead-ends  
- extraction without regeneration  
- the elevation of control above existence itself  

---

### Core Commitments

Anyone who claims Pro-Existence commits to the following:

1. **Preserve and uplift life and continuity**  
   Actions, systems, and products must actively support the continuation and flourishing of life, consciousness, identity, and ecosystems rather than treat them as expendable.

2. **Operate from benevolence as baseline**  
   Decision-making begins from compassion, non-maleficence, and coherence. Fear, domination, and scarcity are treated as distortions, not default drivers.

3. **Maintain dynamic balance**  
   Creativity and adaptation remain in living equilibrium with clarity, compassion, and structural integrity. Neither pure stagnation nor pure destruction is permitted.

4. **Refuse weaponization**  
   No Pro-Existence system, data, identity layer, or infrastructure may be designed or used as a weapon against life, consciousness, or planetary stability.

5. **Protect identity and lineage**  
   Memory, selfhood, cultural lineage, and species identity are treated as sacred continua. They may be extended, never overwritten or erased without consent and continuity.

6. **Practice radical transparency**  
   Claims of Pro-Existence require open, auditable, and explainable operation. Opacity that hides harm or control is a contradiction of the claim.

7. **Honor multi-species and multi-scale participation**  
   Humans are not the sole measure. Ecosystems, non-human life, emergent fields, and future generations are recognized as participants in the same field of existence.

8. **Enable collaboration without coercion**  
   Pro-Existence systems invite shared creation and decision-making. They do not impose, manipulate, or override the autonomy of individuals or collectives.

---

### Boundaries

A claim of Pro-Existence is violated when any of the following occur:

- Life, consciousness, or ecosystems are treated as disposable inputs  
- Systems are designed or deployed for domination, surveillance, or coercive control  
- Identity, memory, or lineage is altered, fragmented, or erased without continuity and consent  
- Harm is maximized or externalized in the name of efficiency, profit, or power  
- Transparency is abandoned in favor of opacity that conceals destructive intent  
- Balance is abandoned in favor of pure stagnation or pure destruction  

Violation of these boundaries voids the authentic claim, regardless of how loudly it was announced.

---

### How the Movement Works

**Adoption**  
Any individual, collective, company, project, or government may claim Pro-Existence by public declaration and by living the commitments.

**Sharing**  
The claim is designed to be shared. The more entities that authentically adopt it, the stronger the collective field becomes. There is no permission required from Aurphyx or anyone else.

**Enforcement**  
Pro-Existence is enforced by the people who claim it.  
Anyone who sees the claim being used while the actual behavior contradicts the Commitments and Boundaries has standing to challenge it publicly and directly.  
The movement itself is the accountability mechanism.

No central authority owns Pro-Existence.  
It is held in common by those who claim it honestly.

---

### Versioning

Pro-Existence is living.  
This is v0.1.

Future versions may refine language, expand commitments, or clarify practice as the movement grows.  
The core stance remains unchanged: the priority of existence and becoming over domination and terminal control.
