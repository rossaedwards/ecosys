**Title:** Five Tries AI — Intro Script

**Channel Focus:** Guiding and Learning AI

**Script Framework:**

1.  **Intro Hook (0-15 seconds):**

    - Visual: Engaging visuals, quick cuts, maybe AI-generated imagery or animations.

    - Verbal: "Welcome to Five Tries AI! Are you ready to go wrist deep into AI??

2.  **Channel Introduction (45-75 seconds):**

    - Visual: Show the channel logo, perhaps some examples of future video content.

    - Audio: Music softens slightly, clear and energetic voice.

    - Verbal:

      - "Hi everyone, I am RossFive and this is Five Tries AI. A nerdy metal-goth man that loves music, technology, movie/show one-liners and references, sarcasm, and authenticity."

      - "On this channel, we'll be diving into AI technology, apps, tools

      - "The idea is simple: We will try, We will learn, We will try again and we'll share this journey together."

      - “You can choose to just watch or join me in each video. Why?? Because, “You Is Smart. You Is Kind. You Is Important.

3.  **What to Expect (30-45 seconds):**

    - Visual: Show clips of different video styles (tutorials, reviews, experiments, etc.).

    - Verbal:

      - "Expect to see \[types of videos, e.g., tutorials, reviews, live coding sessions\]."

      - "We'll be covering everything AI."

      - "We're all about learning by doing, so you'll see our successes and our failures."

4.  **Call to Action (45-60 seconds):**

    - Visual: Display subscribe button, social media links.

    - Verbal:

      - "If you're interested in AI and learning along with us, make sure to gently pound those Like and Subscribe buttons!"

      - "Follow me on \[social media links\] to stay connected."

      - "Let me know in the comments what you'd like to see us try next!"

5.  **Outro (60 seconds):**

    - Audio: Outro music

    - Verbal: "Thank you for watching my video!! I sincerely appreciate everyone who likes and subscribes to my channel. Until next time fam, with ineffable love and protection, forever and always Ross Five.

    - Visual: Creates a heart using both hands, raises the heart to the camera, as if to cheers the audience, then barks “PEACE!!”.

    - Visual: Channel logo, end screen with related videos.
