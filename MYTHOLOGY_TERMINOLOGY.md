## 1. Kill-shots (never in APS prose)

Do **not** use these as operating claims in TVFD, VIM, FTQC, rÆ datasheets, or matrices:

| Kill-shot | Why it dies |
|---|---|
| Infinite available energy / infinite vacuum energy | Singular limit ≠ operating point |
| Perpetual / endless supply (no drive cost) | Erases drive + regulation bill |
| Free ZPE / free energy from equilibrium vacuum | Violates detailed balance / 2nd law framing |
| Battery-free unlimited range (no power accounting) | Brochure physics |
| Perfect resonance as stable run mode | Model says runaway / loss of control |
| Vaporizing lattice / destroying spacetime as feature | Failure-mode poetry only |

**Allowed failure-mode use:** “If the governor fails and match is forced to the singular limit, the model predicts runaway flux and lattice loss.”  
That is a **warning**, not a product feature.
