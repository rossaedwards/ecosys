\section{APS-OKF}\label{aps-okf}

\textbf{Aurphyx Primordial Standard --- Object Knowledge Frontmatter}\\
Version 1.0.0 · Status stable\\
Machine contract: \href{./aps-okf.yaml}{\texttt{aps-okf.yaml}}\\
Aurphyx-internal law: \href{./APS_OKF_SCHEMA.md}{\texttt{APS\_OKF\_SCHEMA.md}}

Format license: \textbf{Apache-2.0}\\
Implement, validate, emit, and ship this header in any project.\\
Document bodies keep whatever license they already have.

\textbf{This format is APS-OKF. It is not Vibe.}\\
Vibe / VASP is the audio standard and player. Do not name this header Vibe-OKF.

\subsection{1. What this is}\label{what-this-is}

A document without a header is text. A document with APS-OKF is an object.

The header answers seven questions before anyone reads the body:

{\def\LTcaptype{none} % do not increment counter
\begin{longtable}[]{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule\noalign{}
Question & Key \\
\midrule\noalign{}
\endhead
\bottomrule\noalign{}
\endlastfoot
What kind of object is this? & \texttt{type} \\
What is it called? & \texttt{title} \\
What is it, in one sentence? & \texttt{description} \\
Who owns the source? & \texttt{workspaces} \\
What runs or consumes it? & \texttt{services} \\
What subject does it govern? & \texttt{domains} \\
Where does it sit on the lattice? & \texttt{nodes}, \texttt{cores}, \texttt{fields} \\
\end{longtable}
}

Nine keys. Fixed order. No extras in v1.

This is the public formalization of the TSLCA frontmatter. Aurphyx APS documents are the \texttt{aps-tslca} profile of this contract. \href{./APS_OKF_SCHEMA.md}{\texttt{APS\_OKF\_SCHEMA.md}} remains the Aurphyx-internal law. This file is the public spec.

\subsection{4. The nine keys}\label{the-nine-keys}

Order is law. Emit keys in this sequence.

\midrule\noalign{}
\endhead
\bottomrule\noalign{}

\texttt{type} & Kind of object. Must be a registry value. \\
\texttt{title} & Human-readable name. \\
\texttt{description} & One-sentence scope. \\
\texttt{workspaces} & Source ownership. Aurphyx default: \texttt{rossaedwards/ecosys,\ aurphyx/ecosys}. Lab first, publish repo second. Do not push from this lab task. \\
\texttt{services} & What runs or consumes the object. \\
\texttt{domains} & Subject, company domains, folders, or repos. \\
\texttt{nodes} & Lattice cells. TSLCA writes \texttt{CORE⊗CORE}. \\
\texttt{cores} & Basis triad. TSLCA locks SIX, SCX, ICX. \\
\texttt{fields} & Named fields, regions, markets, or industries. \\
\end{longtable}
}

\texttt{additionalProperties} is false. Extra keys fail v1.

Invariants for both profiles:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Every node side must be a listed core.
\item
  A full 3×3 is written row-major.
\item
  Tensor products are non-commutative.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{7. Application law}\label{application-law}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Prepend the header. Do not rewrite the body.
\item
  Skip binaries.
\item
  Do not stamp unrelated folders as a side effect of adopting this spec.
\item
  \texttt{rossaedwards/ecosys} is the lab. \texttt{aurphyx/ecosys} is the later publish repo.
\item
  Document bodies keep their existing license. The format license is Apache-2.0.
\end{enumerate}
