## 5. The Nine Pillars of Somatic Continuity

The nine pillars form a spectrum from controlled entry to extended felt presence. Full-domain claims require all nine.

### 5.1 The Gate

The identity-controlled aperture that admits or rejects incoming sensorimotor or interoceptive content before it becomes full experience.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST support identity-configurable Gates for major sensory and interoceptive channels.
- MUST log Gate state changes that affect high-stakes experience.
- SHALL treat unauthorized Gate override as a continuity violation.

### 5.2 The Frame

The contextual and attentional window through which experience is organized: what is foreground, what is background, what is currently relevant to the identity.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST allow identity-conditioned Framing of perceptual fields.
- MUST preserve the capacity to re-frame without permanent loss of prior Frames.
- SHOULD support multi-scale Framing (moment, episode, life-chapter).

### 5.3 The Habit

The learned, identity-shaped patterns of attention, posture, response, and filtering that give experience its characteristic “feel” for this vessel.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST treat Habits as first-class, updatable objects under identity control.
- MUST preserve provenance of Habit formation and modification.
- SHALL allow Habit interruption or re-training without identity discontinuity.

### 5.4 The Threshold

The intensity, salience, or relevance level at which content crosses from latent to experienced under current identity state and Gate settings.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST expose Threshold parameters for major channels where technically feasible.
- MUST prevent silent Threshold manipulation that would hide or force experience against consent.
- SHOULD support dynamic Threshold adjustment under explicit identity direction.

### 5.5 The Echo

The residual, after-effect, or memory-trace of recent experience that continues to color present perception under identity continuity.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST distinguish Echo from pure Morphic Memory Traces (short-term experiential residue vs. long-term provenance).
- MUST allow Echo decay and clearing under identity control.
- SHALL not allow Echo to permanently override current Gate or Frame settings without consent.

### 5.6 The Attitude

The standing evaluative and postural orientation of the identity toward incoming experience: open, guarded, curious, defensive, etc.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST treat Attitude as a first-class modulator of the Experiential Filter.
- MUST keep Attitude under identity ownership and inspection.
- SHOULD integrate with Affective channels from Language and with SAGES ethical posture.

### 5.7 The Aperture

The current effective openness of the sensorimotor system under identity control — how much of the available field is actually being lived right now.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST support Aperture reporting and adjustment by the identity-bearing party.
- MUST prevent external systems from forcing Aperture closed or open against consent.
- SHALL log significant Aperture changes that affect continuity or safety.

### 5.8 The Viscera

The deep, interoceptive, and felt-sense layers of experience: gut, breath, heart, pressure, temperature, and the pre-reflective body sense that anchors identity in the living vessel.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST treat Visceral channels as first-class continuity carriers, not as secondary “noise.”
- MUST protect Visceral data under the same consent and Horizon rules as other identity material.
- SHOULD interoperate with Vessel Binding’s Print, Cadence, and Resonance pillars.

### 5.9 The Aura

The extended, felt boundary of the living vessel as experienced from within — the sense of “where I end and the world begins” under current identity state. Distinct from but complementary to the Perimeter of Vessel Binding.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST allow the identity to report and, where possible, modulate felt Aura boundaries.
- MUST treat non-consensual Aura intrusion or collapse as a continuity and Non-Maleficence violation.
- SHALL align Aura handling with SAGES Reciprocity and Identity Continuity.

## 6. Normative Requirements (System-Level)

A conformant Somatic Continuity implementation **MUST**:

1. Realize all nine pillars or explicitly declare a partial claim with justification.
2. Preserve the directed mapping ICX → SIX (identity continuity into modulated perception).
3. Enforce experiential continuity and Gate / Threshold / Aura constraints under SAGES Identity Continuity.
4. Require explicit consent and non-decreasing accessibility weight for identity-relevant filtering operations.
5. Maintain provenance of significant Gate, Habit, and Threshold changes via AuraFS / GuardHash / SoulCrypt.
6. Treat Visceral and Aura layers as protected identity surfaces.
7. Abort any trajectory that would invent, erase, or force experience against the identity’s continuity claim without Renewal path.

A conformant implementation **SHALL NOT**:

- Allow external systems to override Gates, Thresholds, or Aperture against consent.
- Treat interoceptive / Visceral channels as disposable or purely instrumental.
- Permit silent accumulation of Echo or Habit that permanently distorts identity-relevant perception without disclosure.

- Provenance of filter-state changes remains intact.
- SAGES invariants (especially Identity Continuity and Non-Maleficence) remain intact after filtering operations.
