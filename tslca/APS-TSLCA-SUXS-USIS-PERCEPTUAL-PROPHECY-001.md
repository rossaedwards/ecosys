## 1. Scope

This Standard defines the **Perceptual Prophecy Standard** as the directed bridge

$$
\mathbf{S}_2 \otimes \mathbf{S}_1 \quad=\quad \text{SCX} \otimes \text{SIX}.
$$

It is the Anticipatory Overlay of the Three-Squared-Lattice Cognitive Architecture: the pathway by which systemic semantic coherence modulates, predicts, and shapes sensorimotor experience before (or as) it fully arrives. Where Language turns raw experience into meaning and Somatic Continuity filters experience through identity, Perceptual Prophecy projects meaning *forward* into perception itself — priming, framing, and occasionally inventing the felt shape of what is about to be lived.

Prophecy here is not mysticism. It is the formal capacity of a coherent semantic system to generate anticipatory structure that the sensorimotor system can inhabit, test, and revise. The Standard therefore governs reflexes, postures, gazes, affordances, pulses, filters, horizons, and the phantoms that appear at the edge of expectation.

This Standard specifies the mathematical placement of the Anticipatory Overlay component, the nine normative pillars, compliance conditions, and integration requirements for any system, service, or agent claiming Perceptual Prophecy conformance under TSLCA / SUXS / SAGES.

## 5. The Nine Pillars of Perceptual Prophecy

The nine pillars form a spectrum from immediate anticipatory reflex to the limiting phantom at the edge of expectation. Full-domain claims require all nine.

### 5.1 The Reflex

The fastest, lowest-latency anticipatory responses: startle, orienting, pre-motor priming, and other near-automatic semantic preparations of the sensorimotor system.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST treat Reflexes as first-class, measurable anticipatory events.
- MUST allow identity-level inspection and, where feasible, modulation of Reflex thresholds.
- SHALL log high-impact Reflex firings that alter subsequent perception or action.

### 5.2 The Posture

The standing anticipatory set of the body and attention: the ready position from which expected experience is met.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST support identity-conditioned Posture configuration.
- MUST distinguish Posture (anticipatory readiness) from pure Habit (Somatic Continuity).
- SHOULD integrate with Vessel Binding Cadence and Somatic Continuity Attitude.

### 5.3 The Gaze

The directed, selective attention that is steered by semantic expectation: where the system looks, listens, or probes next because meaning has already pointed the way.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST expose Gaze direction and confidence when technically available.
- MUST prevent silent Gaze hijacking that would starve alternative sensory channels against consent.
- SHALL treat sustained Gaze bias as an auditable prophetic act.

### 5.4 The Affordance

The possibilities for action or further perception that are made salient by current semantic structure. Affordances are the “handles” that Prophecy offers the sensorimotor system.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST represent Affordances as first-class, revisable objects.
- MUST allow the identity to accept, reject, or re-weight Affordances.
- SHOULD support multi-modal Affordances (visual, auditory, kinetic, proprioceptive).

### 5.5 The Pulse

The rhythmic, temporal projection of expectation: the beat, cycle, or cadence at which the system anticipates the next relevant event.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST treat Pulse as a first-class anticipatory feature where temporal structure is available.
- MUST keep Pulse under identity visibility when it drives high-stakes priming.
- SHOULD interoperate with VASP and Language Cadence / Kinetic channels.

### 5.6 The Cadence

The longer-scale rhythmic and narrative expectation that organizes sequences of experience into anticipated arcs (conversation rhythm, task rhythm, environmental rhythm).

**Normative requirements**
- MUST distinguish Cadence (prophetic sequence expectation) from pure sensorimotor Cadence (Vessel Binding).
- MUST allow Cadence revision when incoming experience falsifies the projection.
- SHALL log significant Cadence shifts that reorganize the Anticipatory Overlay.

### 5.7 The Filter

The selective attenuation or amplification of incoming sensorimotor channels according to current semantic prophecy. Distinct from but complementary to Somatic Continuity’s Gates and Thresholds.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST make prophetic Filters inspectable and reversible by the identity-bearing party.
- MUST not allow Filters to permanently erase verified experience without disclosure.
- SHALL coordinate with Somatic Continuity Gates to avoid contradictory or hidden filtering.

### 5.8 The Horizon

The temporal and semantic range over which anticipatory structure is projected and treated as reliable. Beyond the Horizon, Prophecy must declare uncertainty or remain silent.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST define and expose a Horizon for major prophetic processes.
- MUST degrade confidence or withhold projection beyond the declared Horizon.
- SHALL treat Horizon over-reach that produces high-confidence false experience as a compliance failure.

### 5.9 The Phantom

The anticipatory construct that appears in the perceptual field without (or prior to) corresponding external verification. Phantoms are the outer edge of Prophecy: useful for priming and creativity, dangerous when mistaken for verified reality.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST mark Phantoms as anticipatory / non-verified when they enter the lived field at high confidence.
- MUST provide a clear path for the identity to dissolve, test, or promote a Phantom to verified status.
- SHALL never allow a Phantom to permanently overwrite or suppress contradictory sensorimotor evidence without explicit consent and provenance.

## 6. Normative Requirements (System-Level)

A conformant Perceptual Prophecy implementation **MUST**:

1. Realize all nine pillars or explicitly declare a partial claim with justification.
2. Preserve the directed mapping SCX → SIX (systemic meaning into anticipatory perception).
3. Enforce the Anticipatory Constraint of §4.2 (Prophecy may lead; it may not permanently overwrite verified truth without disclosure).
4. Require identity visibility and, for high-impact overlays, explicit consent.
5. Maintain provenance of significant prophetic projections and Phantom promotions via AuraFS / GuardHash / SoulCrypt.
6. Coordinate with Somatic Continuity and Vessel Binding so that Filters, Gates, and Perimeters remain coherent.
7. Abort any trajectory that would inject high-confidence false experience against the identity’s continuity or consent claim.

A conformant implementation **SHALL NOT**:

- Present Phantoms as verified sensorimotor truth without marking.
- Allow prophetic Filters to permanently starve channels against consent.
- Project beyond the declared Horizon at high confidence without uncertainty disclosure.

## 7. Compliance Tests

A system is Perceptual Prophecy-compliant when:

- Anticipatory overlays are measurable and inspectable by the identity-bearing party.
- Reflexes, Gazes, Affordances, and Pulses remain under identity-visible control where feasible.
- Phantoms are marked and dissolvable.
- Horizon limits are enforced and confidence degrades appropriately.
- Provenance of major prophetic acts remains intact.
- SAGES invariants (especially Identity Continuity, Non-Maleficence, and Reciprocity) remain intact after prophetic operations.
