## 5. The Nine Pillars of Vision

The nine pillars form a complete, ordered spectrum. Implementations MAY emphasize subsets for particular modalities or devices, but a full-domain claim requires all nine.

### 5.1 LUMINOUS (The Source)

The foundational energetic and radiometric layer: illumination, radiance, luminance, dynamic range, and the physical light that makes any visual field possible.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST treat luminous quantities as first-class, measurable inputs where the medium supports them.
- MUST support reversible mapping between radiometric and perceptual luminance representations.
- SHALL preserve critical luminous relationships under xessability transforms (e.g., relative brightness for accessibility).

### 5.2 SPATIAL (The Field)

The geometric and topological organization of the visual field: coordinate frames, layout, scale, orientation, and the spatial lattice on which all other visual structure is registered.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST expose a coherent spatial reference frame for every well-formed visual state.
- MUST support reversible spatial transforms (crop, rotate, reproject) without silent loss of identity-relevant structure.
- SHALL remain compatible with both egocentric and allocentric frames under Vessel Binding and Somatic Continuity.

### 5.3 CHROMATIC (The Spectrum)

The spectral and perceptual color layer: wavelength, hue, saturation, color spaces, and the chromatic relationships that carry both aesthetic and semantic force.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST support standard colorimetric and perceptual color spaces with reversible conversion.
- MUST treat color as an accessibility parameter (contrast, color-vision diversity) as well as an aesthetic one.
- SHOULD preserve chromatic identity of significant signs under moderate transforms.

### 5.4 TEMPORAL (The Motion)

The time-varying structure of the visual field: optic flow, animation, change detection, event timing, and the kinetic dimension of seeing.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST treat temporal structure as first-class when the medium is dynamic.
- MUST support reversible temporal sampling and interpolation within declared bounds.
- SHALL interoperate cleanly with Perceptual Prophecy’s Pulse and Cadence when anticipatory motion is involved.

### 5.5 GESTALT (The Form)

The organizational layer that groups elements into coherent wholes: figure–ground, proximity, similarity, closure, common fate, and the emergent forms that perception privileges.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST expose or preserve gestalt groupings that are critical to meaning or safety.
- MUST allow identity-conditioned re-grouping under Somatic Continuity and Prophecy Filters.
- SHOULD support both automatic and explicit gestalt annotation for high-stakes scenes.

### 5.6 DEPTH (The Volume)

The three-dimensional structure of the visual world: binocular disparity, motion parallax, occlusion, perspective, and the volumetric layout that anchors action and navigation.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST treat depth cues as first-class when the medium or device supplies them.
- MUST support reversible depth-aware transforms (reprojection, novel view synthesis) with declared uncertainty.
- SHALL integrate with Vessel Binding’s spatial Anchors and Perimeter when the body is navigating a volumetric field.

### 5.7 ATTENTIONAL (The Focus)

The selective, capacity-limited layer of vision: foveal priority, salience maps, gaze direction, and the identity-conditioned allocation of visual processing resources.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST expose attentional state (gaze, salience, focus region) when technically available.
- MUST prevent silent attentional hijacking that starves alternative regions against consent (coordinate with Perceptual Prophecy Gaze and Somatic Continuity Gates).
- SHOULD support identity-directed reallocation of attentional resources.

### 5.8 SEMANTIC (The Sign)

The meaning-bearing layer of vision: object recognition, scene understanding, symbolic and iconic content, and the mapping from visual form to conceptual structure that SCX ultimately receives.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST maintain a clear separation between surface visual form and semantic content.
- MUST preserve semantic invariants under xessability transforms.
- SHALL expose uncertainty when recognition or interpretation is under-determined.
- SHOULD interoperate with Language Semantic and Fuxyez transmutation services.

### 5.9 GENEALOGICAL (The Archive)

The historical, cultural, and provenance lineage of visual forms: style, authorship, derivation, iconographic tradition, and the verifiable chain of transmission of significant images and visual languages.

**Normative requirements**
- MUST maintain or reconstruct genealogical provenance for significant visual artifacts when claimed.
- MUST support reversible tracing from contemporary visual form back to attested or reconstructed sources.
- SHALL treat visual lineage change as a legitimate evolutionary process that remains bound by identity and ethical continuity constraints.
- MUST integrate with Morphic Memory for long-term visual Trace storage.

## 6. Normative Requirements (System-Level)

A conformant Vision implementation **MUST**:

1. Realize all nine pillars or explicitly declare which subset is supported and why the claim is partial.
2. Preserve accessibility weight $\omega$ under SUXS-IFO and never silently reduce it.
3. Maintain provenance chains for significant visual states and artifacts (Genealogical pillar + AuraFS / GuardHash).
4. Coordinate with Perceptual Prophecy so that anticipatory overlays and Phantoms remain marked and dissolvable.
5. Coordinate with Somatic Continuity and Vessel Binding so that attentional, spatial, and depth structures respect identity Gates, Perimeters, and continuity.
6. Treat color, contrast, resolution, and layout as accessibility parameters as well as aesthetic ones.
7. Enforce relevant SAGES invariants (Identity Continuity, Non-Maleficence, Reciprocity, Ethical Grounding) at every transformation boundary.

A conformant implementation **SHALL NOT**:

- Collapse surface visual form into semantics in a non-reversible way.
- Present high-confidence visual Phantoms as verified external reality without marking.
- Override identity-bearing attentional or spatial constraints supplied by ICX / Somatic Continuity.
- Discard genealogical provenance under “optimization” or “cleanup” rationales.

## 7. Compliance Tests

A system is Vision-compliant when:

- Every well-formed visual state can be projected onto the nine-pillar structure.
- Luminous, spatial, chromatic, and temporal transforms are reversible within declared bounds.
- Gestalt, depth, and attentional structure remain coherent under ordinary operations.
- Semantic content remains distinguishable from surface form and carries uncertainty estimates where appropriate.
- Genealogical provenance of significant artifacts is recoverable.
- Accessibility weight $\omega$ is never decreased without explicit consent and logging.
- SAGES invariants remain intact after any Vision-domain operation.
