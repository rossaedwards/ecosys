## VASP Project Summary

```markdown
# Vibe Audio Standard and Protocol (VASP)

## Overview

**Vibe Audio Standard and Protocol (VASP), Version 3.69** is an Aurphyx metadata standard for describing the experiential identity of audio.

Traditional music metadata records bibliographic facts such as title, artist, album, genre, and identifiers. VASP expands that model by describing how audio is structured, feels, appears, moves, fits a situation, and connects to cultural lineage.

VASP organizes audio metadata as a complete **nine-pillar profile**. It is designed to support music analysis, visualizers, recommendation systems, live performance environments, creative tools, audio applications, and future Aurphyx ecosystem integrations.

## Core Principle

A complete VASP profile contains:

- `VAP_VERSION`
- `IDENTITY`
- `PILLARS`

`IDENTITY` includes at minimum:

- `TITLE`
- `ARTIST`

`PILLARS` always contains all nine canonical pillar objects:

```json
[
  "STRUCTURAL",
  "TONAL",
  "TIMBRAL",
  "LINGUISTIC",
  "AFFECTIVE",
  "CONTEXTUAL",
  "PHOTOMETRIC",
  "KINETIC",
  "GENEALOGICAL"
]
```

Inner metadata fields may be populated, unknown, pending, or unavailable depending on available source material and analysis confidence.

## The Nine Pillars

| # | Pillar | Archetype | Purpose |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | `STRUCTURAL` | The Skeleton | Tempo, time signature, rhythmic behavior, arrangement, and percussive DNA |
| 2 | `TONAL` | The Flesh | Key, harmony, melody, dissonance, pitch, and tuning |
| 3 | `TIMBRAL` | The Skin | Spectral balance, fidelity, spatial character, production aesthetic, and texture |
| 4 | `LINGUISTIC` | The Voice | Lyrics, semantic content, vocal style, language, and explicit-content classification |
| 5 | `AFFECTIVE` | The Heart | Valence, arousal, dominance, emotional complexity, and tension movement |
| 6 | `CONTEXTUAL` | The Scene | Scenario, setting, activity, intent, time of day, weather, and environmental match |
| 7 | `PHOTOMETRIC` | The Eye | Color palette, visual texture, brightness, lighting behavior, and synchronization output |
| 8 | `KINETIC` | The Body | Entrainment, movement response, energy expenditure, and physical activity metadata |
| 9 | `GENEALOGICAL` | The Roots | Era, sampling lineage, genre tree, cultural context, and tribe alignment |

## VASP Visualizer

The VASP Visualizer is an Android-first creative application that transforms audio activity and VASP metadata into an interactive visual scene.

The application combines two layers:

1. **Live audio layer**
   - Waveform
   - Frequency spectrum
   - Amplitude
   - Beat pulse
   - Energy response

2. **VASP metadata layer**
   - Visual palette
   - Scene intensity
   - Motion character
   - Particle density
   - Geometry
   - Texture
   - Contextual preset behavior

The `PHOTOMETRIC` pillar is the primary controller for color, lighting, fades, and visual texture. Other pillars contribute creative influences to motion, geometry, density, energy, and scene behavior.

VASP visual mappings are creative metadata mappings. They are not medical, psychological, biometric, or scientific diagnoses.
