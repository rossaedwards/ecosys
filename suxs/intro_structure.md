To maintain coherence and versioning, the Standards will be generated in the canonical order:

APS‑SIS‑001 — Symbiotic Integration Standards

APS‑SCS‑001 — Systemic Coherence Standards

APS‑ICX‑001 — Identity‑Coherence Standards

APS-USIS-001 - Universal Soul Identity Standards

APS‑SUXS‑001 — Symbiotic Universal Xessability Standards (integrator)

Each Standard will follow a consistent structure:

1. Scope

2. Normative References

3. Definitions

4. Mathematical Model

5. Normative Requirements (MUST/SHALL/SHOULD)

6. Compliance Tests

7. Security & Governance Considerations

8. Integration Notes

9. Versioning & Extensibility
