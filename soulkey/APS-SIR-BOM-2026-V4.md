## Strategic Intent

We are purchasing **one high-quality dark architectural slab** and cutting multiple form factors from it.  

This single material acquisition will produce:

- Hero **Ancient Tablet SIRs**
- Multiple **Pocket / Handheld SIRs**
- **Pendant blanks**
- Thin stock for **earrings, rings, cuff elements**, and future experimental pieces

The goal is maximum learning and presence from the first physical stone while controlling cost and preserving material for iteration.

**Primary Material Target (in priority order):**
1. Absolute Black Gabbro / Premium Absolute Black (most practical for multi-yield)
2. Spectrolite-grade Labradorite (for living flash)
3. Imperial Black / Dark Forest Nephrite Jade (ideal toughness & aesthetic, higher cost & rarer in large slabs)

## Recommended Cutting Sequence (Fabricator)

1. Inspect full slab for best aesthetic zones (color uniformity, absence of cracks).
2. Cut large **Tablet** blanks first from the highest-quality regions.
3. Cut **Pocket** blanks from remaining good areas.
4. Slice remaining material into **Pendant** and **Thin Stock** thicknesses.
5. Machine pockets and raised borders only on the pieces selected for full finishing.
6. Hold back a significant amount of un-machined stock for future design iteration.

---

## Critical Quality Attributes (All Form Factors)

- Dimensional tolerance: ±0.15 mm on finished outer dimensions
- Pocket floor flatness: ±0.08 mm
- High-luster polish on all show faces
- Zero visible tool marks or subsurface micro-fracture
- Clean, chip-free edges and raised borders
